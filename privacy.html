Who is responsible

Michal Maxian runs this site and is the controller for the data described here. Contact: michal@maxian.sk.

Sharing a secret

The site’s own code stores no accounts, cookies or IP addresses. The page where secrets are created and opened sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other sites. Cloudflare, which hosts the site, handles connections (IP address, request details) as any host does. What is stored for a secret is described in how it works.

Google Analytics

Google Analytics runs only on the information pages (how it works, command line and this page), and only after you press Accept. It never runs on the page where secrets are created or opened, and secret links are never sent to it: the page address it receives has no part after ? or #.

  • Provider: Google Ireland Limited.
  • Purpose: counting visits to the information pages.
  • What Google receives: after you accept, Google receives your IP address, the page visited, and browser and device details, and the cookies identify your browser with a random id. This is personal data, not anonymous.
  • Cookies: _ga and _ga_<ID>, set by Google Analytics after you accept. They last up to 12 months.
  • Retention: analytics data is kept for 2 months (a setting in the Google Analytics account).
  • Transfer: data may be processed in the USA by Google, under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • Legal basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Cookie settings and withdrawing consent

The banner on the information pages offers Accept and Reject, and Reject is the same size as Accept. Nothing is sent to Google before you accept, or if you reject. To change your choice, use Cookie settings, here or in the footer of any page. Rejecting deletes the Google Analytics cookies on this site. The page is reloaded only if the Google script had already been loaded on it (after an earlier Accept), because a loaded script cannot be unloaded. You are asked again after 12 months or when these purposes change. Without JavaScript nothing is loaded and no banner is shown.

What is saved in your browser

  • Your theme choice (light or dark), in local storage.
  • Your cookie choice (accepted or rejected, the date and a version number), in local storage. It never leaves your browser.
  • The Google Analytics cookies above, only if you accepted.

Share a secret

No accounts. Secrets are encrypted on your device. The page where secrets are created and opened has no analytics or cookies. This page uses Google Analytics only if you accept. Your theme choice and this choice are saved in your browser.
