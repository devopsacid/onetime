What onetime is

onetime is a free service for sharing a secret, such as a password, a token or a small file, through a link that works once. It also has a password, token and UUID generator that runs only in your browser. There are no accounts.

It is run by Michal Maxian, in Slovakia. These terms apply to onetimepass.pages.dev, where secrets are created and opened, and to this information site.

What you must not send

Do not use onetime to send or store:

  • illegal content of any kind;
  • malware, or any other code meant to damage or take over devices or data;
  • child sexual abuse material;
  • content that infringes other people’s rights, such as copyright, trademarks or privacy.

Do not use it to harass, threaten, deceive or defraud anyone either, for example with phishing links or fake payment details. These rules apply even though nobody but the recipient can read what you send.

Nobody can read what you send

Every secret and file is encrypted in your browser (AES-256-GCM) before it is sent. The key is only in the link, in the part after #, which browsers do not send to the server. So the operator cannot read, scan or recognise any secret or file, including a file’s name and type. Details are in how it works.

How reports are handled

Anyone can report a link that is used for illegal content or abuse. What a report must contain, and where to send it, is under Reporting abuse on the privacy page.

  • On a valid report, the stored item is deleted by its id, without opening it. The key is never asked for and never used.
  • A link that was already opened or has expired is no longer stored, so there is nothing left to delete.
  • There are no accounts, and the site’s code keeps no contact details or IP addresses of the people who create links. So the sender of a deleted item cannot be told about the deletion, and there is no way to appeal against it.
  • Cloudflare D1, the database, keeps its own recovery history for about 7 days. It holds only encrypted data, never keys, so a deleted item cannot be read from it either.
  • The person who sent the report gets a reply about the decision.

No general monitoring

The operator does not watch, scan or filter what people send, and could not, because everything is encrypted. An item is deleted early only after a report or an order from an authority. Otherwise it is deleted when the recipient presses Reveal, or removed after it expires, on a later request to the service.

Limits

  • Texts up to 16 KB. The sender picks 1 hour, 1 day or 7 days.
  • One file per link, up to 256 KB. A file link lasts 1 hour.
  • A link works once: pressing Reveal deletes it. A wrong passphrase deletes it too.
  • Storage is limited: at most 20,000 secrets at once, and a limited total size. While it is nearly full, new links can last at most 1 day; when it is full, creating links fails until space frees up.

Availability and the code

The service is free and provided as it is. There is no guarantee that it is available, that a link can be created or opened, or that an item stays stored until it expires. It can be changed, limited or stopped at any time. Do not use a onetime link as the only copy of anything you need.
