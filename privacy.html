Privacy

Last updated 20 November 2026.

Who is responsible

Michal Maxian runs this site and is the controller for the data described here. Contact: michal@maxian.sk.

Two sites

onetime runs as two separate websites. onetimepass.pages.dev is where secrets are created and opened; it also serves the command-line page, the command-line downloads and the API. It runs no analytics, sets no cookies and loads nothing from other sites, on any page. onetimepass-info.pages.dev is this information site (how it works and this page); it uses Google Analytics only after you accept. Each site saves your theme choice separately. Google Analytics cookies that an earlier version of the secret site set on onetimepass.pages.dev are deleted there on your next visit.

Sharing a secret

The code of both sites stores no accounts, cookies or IP addresses. The secret site (onetimepass.pages.dev) sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other sites. Cloudflare, which hosts both sites, handles connections (IP address, request details) as any host does. What is stored for a secret is described in how it works.

Files are stored the same way, as encrypted data that cannot be read without the key in the link. Their name, type and contents are encrypted in your browser. The stored data shows whether it is a file or a text, and its size class (for a file: up to 1 KB, 4 KB, 16 KB, 64 KB or 128 KB), but not the name, type or contents.

Reporting abuse

If a onetime link is used to send malware or illegal content, write to michal@maxian.sk.

  • Do not send the key. The key is the last part of the link, after the last dot. Remove it before you send the link. The rest of the link holds the id, which is enough to delete it.
  • What is done with a report: a reported link that is still stored is deleted by its id, without opening it.
  • What cannot be done: contents are encrypted end to end, so they cannot be read or scanned without the key. This site’s code keeps no IP addresses or request logs, so it cannot identify the sender. A link that was already opened or has expired is no longer stored and cannot be deleted again.
  • Cloudflare D1 keeps its own recovery history for 7 days (see when the data is deleted). It holds only encrypted data, never keys.

Google Analytics

Google Analytics runs only on the information site (how it works and this page), and only after you press Accept. It never runs on onetimepass.pages.dev, where secrets are created and opened, and secret links are never sent to it: the page address it receives has no part after ? or #.

  • Provider: Google Ireland Limited.
  • Purpose: counting visits to the information site.
  • What Google receives: after you accept, Google receives your IP address, the page visited, and browser and device details, and the cookies identify your browser with a random id. This is personal data, not anonymous.
  • Cookies: _ga and _ga_<ID>, set by Google Analytics after you accept. They last up to 12 months.
  • Retention: analytics data is kept for 2 months (a setting in the Google Analytics account).
  • Transfer: data may be processed in the USA by Google, under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • Legal basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Cookie settings and withdrawing consent

The banner on the information site offers Accept and Reject, and Reject is the same size as Accept. Nothing is sent to Google before you accept, or if you reject. Your choice and the Google Analytics cookies apply to the information site only. To change your choice, use Cookie settings, here or in the footer of any page of the information site. Rejecting deletes the Google Analytics cookies on this site. The page is reloaded only if the Google script had already been loaded on it (after an earlier Accept), because a loaded script cannot be unloaded. You are asked again after 12 months or when these purposes change. Without JavaScript nothing is loaded and no banner is shown.

What is saved in your browser

  • Your theme choice (light or dark), in local storage. Each site saves it separately.
  • Your cookie choice (accepted or rejected, the date and a version number), in local storage of the information site. It never leaves your browser.
  • The Google Analytics cookies above, only if you accepted.

Share a secret

No accounts. Secrets are encrypted on your device. onetimepass.pages.dev, where secrets are created and opened, has no analytics or cookies. This information site uses Google Analytics only if you accept. Your theme choice and this choice are saved in your browser.
