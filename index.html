How your secret is stored

  1. Encrypted in your browser. It is locked with a new random key (AES-256-GCM) before anything is sent.
  2. The key stays in the link. It sits after the #, a part browsers never send, so the server cannot read the secret.
  3. Works once. Pressing “Reveal” hands over the encrypted data and deletes it in the same step.
  4. Nothing about you is kept. Unopened links expire after 1 hour, 1 day or 7 days. No accounts, cookies, analytics or IP addresses.
